Privacy Policy — SoundCloud-NowPlaying

Last updated: September 11, 2026

SoundCloud-NowPlaying is a Twitch Extension that displays the currently playing SoundCloud track in a Twitch panel.

Information processed

When SoundCloud-NowPlaying is running, the companion browser extension detects information about the currently playing SoundCloud track, including:

  • Track title
  • Artist name
  • SoundCloud track URL
  • Track artwork URL, when available
  • Whether a track is currently playing

This information is used solely to display the currently playing track in the Twitch Extension.

How information is used

The currently playing track information is sent to a Cloudflare Worker operated for SoundCloud-NowPlaying. The Worker temporarily stores the current track information so that the Twitch Extension can retrieve and display it.

The extension does not request or store your Twitch username, Twitch password, Twitch authentication credentials, Twitch chat messages, or Twitch identity information.

The extension does not intentionally collect personal information from Twitch viewers.

Third-party services

SoundCloud-NowPlaying uses Cloudflare Workers to transmit and temporarily store the currently playing track information.

The extension also links to SoundCloud when a viewer selects the "Listen on SoundCloud" button. SoundCloud's own privacy practices apply when you visit or use SoundCloud.

Data retention

Only the currently playing track information is stored by the SoundCloud-NowPlaying backend. It is replaced when new track information is received and is not intended to serve as a historical listening database.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or SoundCloud-NowPlaying, please contact the extension author through the contact information provided in the Twitch Extension listing.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality or data handling of SoundCloud-NowPlaying changes.
